## 🧠 Pedagogical Research & AI Integration

Exploring the intersection of modern pedagogy, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity education. These presentations analyze student learning patterns and evaluate frameworks for integrating AI into higher education instruction.

### Constructivist Patterns in Cybersecurity Learning
**[View Presentation ↗](https://gamma.app/docs/Constructivist-Patterns-in-Cybersecurity-Learning-1v7b32a9kx3mev9)**  
An analysis of student essay data from cybersecurity courses, mapping the cognitive evolution of learners as they transition from theoretical understanding to practical practitioner mindsets. This work identifies key "constructivist patterns" that inform curriculum design and assessment strategies.

### AI-Integrated Teaching: Applying Bain's Framework
**[View Presentation ↗](https://gamma.app/docs/Can-AI-Help-Us-Do-What-the-Best-Teachers-Do-lq4dip4njeqxhy7)**  
An evaluation of Generative AI tools through the lens of Ken Bain's seminal work, *What the Best College Teachers Do*. This presentation outlines a framework for leveraging AI to enhance critical thinking, personalize feedback, and foster deep learning without compromising academic integrity or student autonomy.
